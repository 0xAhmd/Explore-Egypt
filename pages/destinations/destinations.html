Popular Destinations

Grand Egyptian Museum

World's Largest Archaeological Museum | Giza, Egypt

Experience the magnificent Grand Egyptian Museum, the world's largest museum dedicated to a single civilization. Located near the Pyramids of Giza, this architectural marvel houses over 100,000 artifacts, including the complete collection from King Tutankhamun's tomb. Discover Egypt's rich history spanning from the Predynastic Period to the Roman era.

King Tutankhamun Collection 100,000+ Artifacts Colossal Statue of Ramses II 52-foot Waziri Papyrus
Explore Museum

Cairo

Capital City | 50+ Attractions

The bustling capital of Egypt, home to ancient pyramids, vibrant markets, and rich Islamic heritage. Explore the Egyptian Museum, Khan el-Khalili bazaar, and historic mosques.

  • Pyramids of Giza
  • Egyptian Museum
  • Islamic Cairo
  • Khan el-Khalili

Luxor

Ancient Thebes | 40+ Attractions

Known as the world's greatest open-air museum, Luxor is home to magnificent temples, royal tombs, and ancient monuments that tell the story of pharaonic Egypt.

  • Valley of the Kings
  • Karnak Temple
  • Luxor Temple
  • Temple of Hatshepsut

Aswan

Nubian City | 25+ Attractions

A peaceful city on the Nile with stunning natural beauty, Nubian culture, and remarkable temples including Abu Simbel and Philae Temple.

  • Abu Simbel Temples
  • Philae Temple
  • Nubian Villages
  • High Dam

Alexandria

Mediterranean Pearl | 30+ Attractions

Egypt's second-largest city, a Mediterranean gem with Greco-Roman history, beautiful coastlines, and the legendary Library of Alexandria.

  • Qaitbay Citadel
  • Bibliotheca Alexandrina
  • Catacombs of Kom el Shoqafa
  • Montaza Palace

Red Sea

Diving Paradise | 20+ Attractions

World-renowned for its crystal-clear waters, vibrant coral reefs, and exceptional diving and snorkeling opportunities. Perfect for beach lovers and water sports enthusiasts.

  • Sharm el-Sheikh
  • Hurghada
  • Dahab
  • Coral Reefs

Sinai Peninsula

Sacred Mountains | 15+ Attractions

A land of dramatic desert landscapes, sacred mountains, and Bedouin culture. Home to Mount Sinai and St. Catherine's Monastery.

  • Mount Sinai
  • St. Catherine's Monastery
  • Colored Canyon
  • Bedouin Culture

Giza Plateau
