Explore Egypt's Attractions

Discover the timeless beauty and rich history of Egypt's most iconic attractions.

Historical Sites

The Pyramids of Giza

Pyramid of Khufu (The Great Pyramid)

A surviving wonder of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World, built as a tomb for Pharaoh Khufu.

The Great Sphinx

A monumental statue with the body of a lion and the head of a human, representing the faithful guardian of the pyramid complex.

Pharaonic Temples

Karnak Temple Complex (Luxor)

A massive collection of temples, buildings, and obelisks, and the largest religious site in the world.

Abu Simbel Temples (Aswan)

Two colossal temples carved into the rock by Ramesses II, an example of brilliant relocation and rescue.

Philae Temple (Aswan)

Dedicated to the goddess Isis, it was entirely relocated to protect it from floodwaters.

Temple of Hatshepsut (Luxor)

A unique three-tiered mortuary temple located in the Valley of the Kings, telling the story of one of Egypt's greatest queens.

Islamic Fortresses and Citadels

The Citadel of Saladin (Cairo)

A historic fortress built by Saladin to protect Cairo from the Crusaders, housing the Mosque of Muhammad Ali.

The Citadel of Qaitbay (Alexandria)

A defensive fortress situated on the site of the ancient Lighthouse of Alexandria, protecting the city from naval invasions.

Museums

Egyptian Museum in Tahrir

Houses the greatest collection of pharaonic artifacts in the world, including the Tutankhamun collection and royal mummies.

National Museum of Egyptian Civilization

Showcases the development of Egyptian civilization through the ages and houses the famous Royal Mummies Hall.

Grand Egyptian Museum

The largest archaeological museum in the world, containing over 100,000 artifacts near the Giza Pyramids.

Nubia Museum

Located in Aswan, it displays the history and culture of ancient Nubia and the life of its people before the High Dam construction.

Alexandria National Museum

Contains artifacts from Pharaonic, Greek, Roman, Islamic, and Coptic eras inside an elegant Italian palace.

Museum of Islamic Art

One of the largest Islamic museums in the world, featuring manuscripts and ceramic and metallic works from various Islamic eras.

Religious Landmarks

Historical Mosques

Mosque of Ibn Tulun (Cairo)

The oldest mosque in Cairo that has preserved its original structure, notable for its unique minaret and vast courtyard.

Al-Azhar Mosque (Cairo)

One of the most important religious and educational institutions in the Islamic world, founded during the Fatimid era.

Muhammad Ali Mosque (The Citadel)

Located inside the Citadel of Saladin, it features Ottoman style and magnificent marble decorations.

Mosque of Sultan Hassan (Cairo)

An Islamic architectural masterpiece from the Mamluk era, considered one of the largest mosques in the world.

Coptic and Ancient Churches

The Hanging Church (Coptic Cairo)

The oldest surviving church in Egypt, named so because it was built atop two towers of the Roman Babylon Fortress.

St. Catherine's Monastery (Sinai)

One of the oldest continuously inhabited Christian monasteries in the world, located at the foot of Mount Sinai, housing a vast historical library.

Church of St. George (Coptic Cairo)

A unique circular Greek Orthodox church, located within the complex of churches in Old Cairo.

Church of St. Sergius and Bacchus (Abu Serga)

Also known as Abu Serga Church, it is believed to have been built over the cave where the Holy Family took refuge.

Synagogues

Ben Ezra Synagogue (Coptic Cairo)

One of the oldest synagogues in Egypt, famous for the discovery of the "Cairo Geniza" documents inside it.

Eliyahu Hanavi Synagogue (Alexandria)

One of the largest and most magnificent synagogues in the Middle East, recently restored as part of Egyptian heritage preservation.
