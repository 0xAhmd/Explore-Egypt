Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa to visit Egypt?

Most visitors require a visa to enter Egypt. You can obtain a visa on arrival at the airport or apply in advance at an Egyptian embassy. Check with your local embassy for specific requirements based on your nationality.

What is the best currency to bring?

Egyptian Pounds (EGP) are the local currency. US Dollars and Euros are also widely accepted in tourist areas. It's recommended to carry some cash as not all places accept cards.

Is Egypt safe for tourists?

Egypt is generally safe for tourists, with millions visiting each year. However, it's important to stay informed about current travel advisories and follow local guidelines and recommendations.

What should I wear when visiting religious sites?

Modest clothing is required at mosques and religious sites. Women should cover their shoulders and knees, and may need to cover their hair. Both men and women should remove shoes before entering mosques.

Can I drink tap water in Egypt?

It's recommended to drink bottled water in Egypt. Avoid tap water and use bottled water for drinking and brushing teeth to prevent any health issues.

What languages are spoken in Egypt?

Arabic is the official language. English is widely spoken in tourist areas, hotels, and restaurants. French is also commonly understood in some areas.
