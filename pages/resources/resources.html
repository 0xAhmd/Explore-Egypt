Travel Tips

Visa & Documentation

  • Most nationalities can obtain a visa on arrival or e-Visa online
  • Passport must be valid for 6 months beyond departure date
  • Keep copies of important documents in separate locations
  • Travel insurance is highly recommended

Health & Safety

  • Drink only bottled water
  • Use high SPF sunscreen and stay hydrated
  • Use reputable transportation services
  • Emergency numbers: Police 122, Ambulance 123

Culture & Etiquette

  • Dress modestly when visiting religious sites
  • Tipping (baksheesh) is customary for most services
  • Bargaining is expected in markets and souks
  • Ask permission before photographing people

Money Matters

  • Currency: Egyptian Pound (EGP)
  • ATMs widely available in major cities
  • Credit cards accepted at hotels and restaurants
  • Budget: $50-100 per day for mid-range travel

Best Time to Visit

  • October to April: cooler weather, ideal for sightseeing
  • May to September: hot weather, fewer crowds
  • Coastal areas pleasant year-round
  • Book accommodations early during peak season

Local Transportation

  • Uber and Careem available in major cities
  • Metro system operates in Cairo
  • Taxis should use meters or agree on fare beforehand
  • Domestic flights connect major tourist destinations
